ℹ️ How YOLO Training Works ▼

Fine-tune the DocLayout-YOLO model with your corrections to improve detection accuracy.

  • Corrections: When you adjust region boundaries or change classes in the Layout Review page, these corrections are stored for training.
  • Minimum Data: We recommend at least 20 pages with corrections for good results. More data = better model.
  • Training Process: The model learns from your corrections to better detect similar regions in the future.
  • Backup: Your current model is automatically backed up before training starts.

Tips for better training:

  • Correct a variety of region types, not just one class
  • Include corrections from different page layouts
  • Be consistent with your corrections
